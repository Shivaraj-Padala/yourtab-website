YourTab

Privacy policy

YourTab is a new tab page for your browser. This page explains what it does with your data.

In short: everything stays in your browser. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. The only thing YourTab sends anywhere is what you type in search, to the search engine you chose, so it can suggest results.

What YourTab stores

YourTab saves these things in your browser's own storage, on your device:

  • Your settings, such as colors, wallpaper, clock, shortcuts and search engine
  • Your notes and to-dos
  • Wallpaper images or videos you add

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We don't run any servers that receive your data.

What YourTab sends

When search suggestions are on, the text you type in the search bar is sent to the search engine you picked (Google, Bing, Brave Search or DuckDuckGo) to get suggestions. The request is sent without cookies. That search engine's own privacy policy applies to it. You can turn suggestions off in Settings, and then YourTab sends nothing.

When you run a search or open a shortcut, your browser goes to that website, just as if you had typed the address.

What YourTab doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
  • No ads and no tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't collect any

Permissions

YourTab asks for the favicon permission to show website icons on your shortcuts, using the icons your browser already has. It also asks to contact the four search engines above, only to fetch search suggestions.

Removing your data

Removing YourTab from your browser deletes everything it stored. You can also delete notes, to-dos and wallpapers inside YourTab at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
